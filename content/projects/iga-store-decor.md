Developed an entire store decor system for independently-owned and operated IGA stores across the country. The challenge was every store was different in terms of layout, ceiling height, etc. The system I developed included standard size signs, a catalog of graphics and images for the owners to choose from to create a look that is consistent across the stores but also allows individual stores to express their own style and local hometown appeal.
